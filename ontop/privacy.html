OnTop — Privacy Policy

Currently, OnTop stores all of your data locally on your device. We do not currently collect, transmit, or share any personal information, and there is no analytics, tracking, or third-party service in the app today.

We may introduce features in the future — such as accounts, cloud backup or cross-device sync — that store your data on our servers. If we offer accounts, you will be able to delete your account and its associated data. We may also collect anonymous, aggregate usage analytics to help us improve the app; this data will never include personal information or be tied to your identity. If we introduce any of these, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and how it is used, and the app will clearly indicate when any such feature is active.

If you have questions, please contact us at james@simplistic-develops.com.
