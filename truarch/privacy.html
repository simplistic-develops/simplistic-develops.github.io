TruArch Design — Privacy Policy

TruArch Design (“the app”) is made by James Truitt, doing business as Simplistic Develops (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects today, what it may collect in future versions, and the choices you have.

What the app collects today

Today, the app collects nothing on its own. The designs you make, your prices and your settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. We never see your designs unless you choose to send them to us.

Information you choose to send us

  • Send feedback. When you use Send feedback, your email app (or your device’s share sheet) opens a message to us. It includes your answers, the app version, your device’s operating system and its version, and a copy of the design you have open. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can edit or remove anything first. We receive your email address and whatever the message contains.
  • Exports and sharing. When you export or share a design, model or cost list, it goes only where you send it.

We use feedback and attached designs to reproduce problems, fix bugs and improve the app. We keep them only as long as they are useful for that, and we delete them if you ask.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test the app through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple shares certain information with us under Apple’s own privacy policy. This includes the name and email address you were invited with, your device model and operating system version, when you installed and used the build, crash reports, and any screenshots and comments you submit through TestFlight. We use this only to run the test and improve the app.

What future versions may collect

We may add the features below. Before any version starts collecting new information, we will update this policy and the app’s privacy details in the App Store and Google Play to say exactly what is collected and why.

  • Analytics and diagnostics. To learn which features are used and to find crashes, the app may collect usage events (such as which screens and tools are used), performance and crash data, your device type and operating system, the app version, and a random identifier for the app installation. We may use a service provider, such as an analytics or crash-reporting service, to process this data for us. We do not use analytics to identify you personally.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Some versions or features may be paid. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card or bank details. We receive a record of what was purchased and whether it is active, so the app can unlock it.
  • Accounts and cloud storage. If we offer optional accounts, backup or syncing, we will store your email address and the designs you choose to store on our servers or our providers’ servers. These features will be opt-in, and you will be able to delete your account and its data from within the app.
  • Advertising. Free versions of the app may show ads served by advertising networks. Ad networks may collect your device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and, only with your permission, your device’s advertising identifier. On iOS, the app will ask your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking across other companies’ apps and websites; if you decline, you may still see ads, but they won’t be personalized using that tracking. Where the law requires consent for personalized ads, we will ask first.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with service providers that process it for us (for example, analytics, crash reporting, cloud hosting or email), under terms that limit their use of it;
  • with advertising networks, if a version of the app shows ads, as described above;
  • with Apple or Google, to handle app distribution, testing and purchases;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Some privacy laws treat sharing information with ad networks for personalized advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. If the app ever does this, you will be able to opt out, either in the app or by emailing us.

Your choices and rights

  • Your designs stay on your device; deleting a design or the app removes them from the device.
  • You can turn off ad tracking at any time in your device settings (on iOS, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU and UK GDPR, and we will honor them. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and storage

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We are based in the United States, and information you send us may be stored and processed there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new date, and for significant changes, especially any new collection of information, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
