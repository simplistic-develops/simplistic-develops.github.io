TruArch Design

Design a house and learn how one goes together. Draw the plan, and TruArch works out what it takes to build it.

What you can do

  1. Draw the planWalls with real dimensions in feet and inches, doors, windows, stairs, roofs, decks and up to three stories.
  2. Choose materialsFlooring, siding, roofing, drywall and finishes, room by room.
  3. See inside the wallsFraming, plumbing, wiring and drywall, worked out from your plan as you draw.
  4. Estimate the costA materials list and cost estimate that updates with every change.
  5. Explore it in 3DOpen the doors, flip the lights on, and see the house in sunlight and shadow.

Honest about its limits

TruArch checks your design against common building-code rules, like room sizes, ceiling heights and bedroom egress windows. Those checks are a guide, not an approval. They don't replace a licensed architect or engineer, or your local permit office. Cost estimates are estimates; prices vary by place and time.

Your data

Today, TruArch needs no account and keeps your designs on your device. It works offline and has no analytics, ads or tracking.

As TruArch grows, future versions may add accounts or store designs online, for things like backup, syncing between devices or sharing. Before that happens, the privacy policy will be updated to say exactly what is stored and why. Read the privacy policy.

Try the beta

TruArch is being tested on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight, including by people who work in the trades. The first public release is planned for this winter.
